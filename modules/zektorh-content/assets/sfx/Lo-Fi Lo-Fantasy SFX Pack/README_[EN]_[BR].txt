SCI-FI SFX PACK
=========================

Created by: Chris Lucas "oGnomo"
Website: ognomo.com.br

Description
-----------
Sound pack created for the jam game Warlord’s Penance (2nd Place Overall, 1-Bit Jam #7). It features combat and exploration sounds with an old-school dungeon crawler feel, but the files may work well for other kinds of projects, specially gritty low fantasy ones. If you’re looking for more enemy sounds, check out my other packs. The sounds combine CC0 samples with synthesized and recorded sounds made by me.
All files are normalized. Further mixing may be required in your engine.

Contents
--------
{No.} audio files, available in OGG and WAV (48k, 24-bit).
File naming follows the UCS (Universal Category System) standard.
